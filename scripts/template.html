How to play

Connect the two titles in six hops or fewer. Each hop needs the two titles to share at least 2 traits.

  • Plan with the target's traits. They're always on show. On your cards, any trait the target also has glows.
  • Pick from a hand of 8 cards. Each shows what it shares with where you are. Reshuffle once per hop, or tap one of the current title's traits to deal only cards with that trait. You can still search every connection.
  • Hot and cold. Cards can show whether they bring you closer: always on Easy, 5 peeks on Medium, 3 on Hard, none on Expert.
  • Lifelines (once each): Radar tells you how many hops are left, Stepping stone deals you a card on a shortest route, Bridge trait shows the trait most routes into the target use.
  • Combos: three hops in a row through three different kinds of trait score +50.
  • From memory hides the cards so you type titles yourself.

Story type

Detective: a mystery title reveals one trait at a time, vaguest first. Guess it in as few clues as possible. Wrong guesses tell you how many traits they share with the answer.

Bridge: five 30-second rounds. Pick the one card that shares 2 traits with both titles. Faster answers score more.

Scoring: 1000 base, +150 for par (+250 under par), +100 for 3+ kinds of trait, +100 for crossing media, +50 for all three media, +50 per combo, +50 for 3+ franchises. −75 per hop over par. Lifelines cost 75, 150 and 100.

New Daily and Detective puzzles arrive at midnight your time. Daily difficulty rotates through the week, par 3 to 6.

Traits come from community tags, movie keywords and 58 keyword connectors that scan game store descriptions and movie plot summaries for shared ideas like time loops, heists, haunted houses and deals with the devil.

Anime data: manami-project anime-offline-database (ODbL). Game data: Steam community tags via the Game Gauntlets catalog. Movies: the IMDb Top 1000 list (Kaggle dataset) with TMDB keywords.
